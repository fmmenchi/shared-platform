# FormDateRangePicker

A labelled date range, already bound to the **form adapter in scope** — the
legend, the two fields, the calendar, the hint and the errors in one tag.

```tsx
<UiProvider adapters={{ form: { field: useMyFormField } }}>
  <FormDateRangePicker
    startName="checkIn"
    endName="checkOut"
    startLabel="Check-in"
    endLabel="Check-out"
    legend="Your stay"
  />
</UiProvider>
```

<Canvas of={FormDateRangePickerStories.Default} />

## It binds two fields, and it is the first here that does

The port is `(name) => BoundField` — a hook called once per field — so two
fields are two calls of it, both unconditional and in a fixed order, exactly as
the rules of hooks require and as the port's contract already asked for. **No
new port shape was needed**, which was worth checking before this was written:
an earlier draft of ADR-0027 claimed the opposite, and the amendment records the
correction.

Everything in
[FormDatePicker](?path=/docs/components-form-adapters-formdatepicker--docs)
applies to each end: the binding sees ISO on the carrier, the user sees their
locale's order, and a routing table keeps native-date-control props off fields
that are not one — run once per end.

## The shell is a Fieldset, not a Field

`Field` gives its control an id and expects one control to take it. Measured
with two, both inputs carried the **same** id, its `<label for>` matched two
elements, and the label named neither — the per-field names win. Two labelled
controls are a group, and a group is named by a **legend**.

axe sees none of that: its duplicate-id rules are deprecated.

## Props

<Controls of={FormDateRangePickerStories.Default} />

Four props `DateRangePicker` has are absent, and each for its own reason.
`defaultStart` and `defaultEnd` are seeds, which are the binding's — given here
they would put a date in the DOM without telling the library, which then
validates an empty field and overwrites it on its first pass. `startCarrierRef`
and `endCarrierRef` are the binding's refs, and there is nothing left to ask
for: this component already merges its own into them.

**Anything a binding produces reaches ONE field.** That distinction is the one
non-obvious thing about composing two bound fields: every prop
`DateRangePicker` takes at the top level reaches **both** inputs, which is right
for `disabled` or `size` and wrong for an `onChange`. Measured before the
per-field channels existed, the start's binding was attached to both carriers
and was told the END's value.

## Usage

### Errors

<Canvas of={FormDateRangePickerStories.WithHintAndError} />

Both ends' messages are shown, in field order, under one group — because a
`Fieldset` is what they can be described by. A library whose messages do not
name their own field ("Required.") will therefore read twice identically. That
is the library's to fix, and papering over it here would mean this component
inventing text nobody wrote.

### Choosing rather than typing

<Canvas of={FormDateRangePickerStories.PickOnly} />

`pickOnly` passes straight through: neither field is typed into, both are the
calendar's trigger, both refuse input at the native `beforeinput` and both
answer Enter, Space and ArrowDown.

## Accessibility

- **The group is named by its legend; each field by its own label.** Three
  names, and none of them borrowed.
- **Two entries, two names** in the `FormData` — the promise the whole family
  is built on, kept for a value that happens to have two halves.
- **A field with no adapter in scope throws** rather than rendering, as
  everywhere else here: an unbound field types perfectly well and submits
  nothing, which is a failure you would meet in production data rather than in
  development.
